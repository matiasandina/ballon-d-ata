# 2025 voting correction

## Decision

The original 2025 addition contained the complete ranking but left every points and percentage value blank. France Football subsequently published the complete voting totals. This correction adds those 30 point totals and calculates percentages from the complete 6,700-point total.

No ranks, player names, clubs, nationalities, positions, or other editions were changed.

## Evidence

[France Football's complete results published by L'Équipe](https://www.lequipe.fr/France-Football/Actualites/Le-classement-complet-du-ballon-d-or-2025-le-triomphe-sans-partage-d-ousmane-dembele/1596939) lists all 30 scores, from Ousmane Dembélé's 1,380 points through Michael Olise's 4 points. It also records the governing ballot rule: 100 journalists each ranked ten players for 15, 12, 10, 8, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 points.

The published scores sum to 6,700, exactly matching 100 complete ballots × 67 points per ballot. [France Football's juror-level voting detail](https://www.lequipe.fr/France-Football/Article/Le-detail-des-votes-pour-le-ballon-d-or-masculin-2025-ousmane-dembele-present-dans-les-votes-de-tous-les-jures/1597021) provides a separate cross-check of the underlying top-ten ballots.

The percentage formula is:

```text
player points / 6,700 × 100
```

Values are rounded to four decimal places, consistent with the dataset's generated percentage precision.
